    How to read the density of this view
    Density comes from how the sample was chosen, not from the Galaxy: out to about 40 pc it holds every Gaia source meeting a parallax cut; beyond that only stars brighter than G = 6.5. Do not read stellar density from this view. Drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, click a star to inspect it. Point size and brightness follow apparent magnitude (a visualisation choice); they do not represent stellar radii. Colour is a BP−RP ramp, not calibrated photometry. The amber dot is the Sun, a reference marker, not a Gaia record.
    Data: ESA Gaia DR3 (ESA/Gaia/DPAC) and SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg). Positions at epoch J2016.0, ICRS. Full acknowledgement and provenance: docs/SCIENTIFIC_DATA.md and docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md in the project repository. Image credits, catalogue and archive acknowledgements, licences.

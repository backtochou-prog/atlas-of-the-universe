# Project Vision

Atlas of the Universe is a scientifically grounded, spatially immersive explorer. A visitor moves from cosmic structure (galaxy clusters, filaments) down to galaxies, star systems, planets and individual objects without the scene becoming unstable.

## Principles
- **Scientific credibility first.** Every displayed value carries source, release and retrieval date. Unknown values are shown as unknown, never estimated silently.
- **Scale is the experience.** Zoom is logarithmic; transitions between scales are continuous.
- **Spectacle serves measurement.** Visual effects must not misrepresent scale, brightness or distance.
- **Platform-ready.** Browser first (desktop), with mobile and WebXR (Meta Quest) kept in view in every architectural decision.

## Experience goals (later phases)
1. Orientation: a visitor always knows where they are in the hierarchy.
2. Continuity: no hard cuts between scales unless explicitly chosen.
3. Discovery: objects are findable by name, designation and coordinates.
4. Honesty: uncertain or hypothetical objects are visibly marked.

## Out of scope for the foundation
Full observable-universe rendering, a finished visual identity, and multiplayer.

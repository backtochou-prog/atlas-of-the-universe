Generated by `tools/discovery/build_bright_stars.ts` on 2026-11-13. Output: `public/data/bright-stars.v1.json.gz` (type `BrightStarFile`, `src/discovery/types.ts`). Rebuild offline: `npx tsx tools/discovery/build_bright_stars.ts --offline` (reads `data/cache/discovery/stars/`).

## Why

Gaia DR3 has no usable astrometry for stars brighter than about G = 1.5, so Sirius, Betelgeuse, Vega, Polaris, Rigel, Arcturus, Alpha Centauri A/B and Antares are not in the atlas's Gaia subset. This file adds the Hipparcos entries for them and for the rest of the naked-eye sky.

## Distance quality

`parallaxQuality` is 'good' when parallax/error >= 10, else 'poor': 7004 good, 1938 poor. Distance is not stored; do not invert 'poor' parallaxes. Betelgeuse-like stars (extended, variable red supergiants) have large uncertainty and resolved discs; the formal error probably understates the real one.

## Gaia de-duplication

Method: for each Hipparcos star propagate the J1991.25 position to J2016.0 with its own proper motion (linear), find Gaia subset records within 3 arcsec, and require parallax agreement within 5 sigma (combined) or 10 percent. The Gaia subset's `hip_id` (Gaia's best neighbour) is tried first; an alternative record is accepted only if unique and passing the same checks.

### Selection criteria

- Bright set: phot_g_mean_mag < 6.5 (all sources, regardless of parallax quality).
- Nearby set: parallax >= 25 mas (distance <= 40 pc) and parallax_over_error >= 5.
- Union of the two sets, one row per source_id, ordered by source_id.
- Cross-matches joined (left outer): gaiadr3.hipparcos2_best_neighbour (Hipparcos number) and gaiadr3.astrophysical_parameters (GSP-Phot Teff, modelled).

### ADQL submitted

```sql
SELECT g.source_id, g.ref_epoch, g.ra, g.ra_error, g.dec, g.dec_error, g.parallax, g.parallax_error, g.pmra, g.pmra_error, g.pmdec, g.pmdec_error, g.phot_g_mean_mag, g.phot_g_mean_flux_over_error, g.phot_bp_mean_mag, g.phot_bp_mean_flux_over_error, g.phot_rp_mean_mag, g.phot_rp_mean_flux_over_error, g.ruwe, g.astrometric_params_solved, g.visibility_periods_used, g.astrometric_excess_noise, g.duplicated_source, h.original_ext_source_id AS hip_id, h.angular_distance AS hip_sep_arcsec, a.teff_gspphot, a.teff_gspphot_lower, a.teff_gspphot_upper
FROM gaiadr3.gaia_source AS g
LEFT OUTER JOIN gaiadr3.hipparcos2_best_neighbour AS h ON g.source_id = h.source_id
LEFT OUTER JOIN gaiadr3.astrophysical_parameters AS a ON g.source_id = a.source_id
WHERE g.phot_g_mean_mag < 6.5 OR (g.parallax >= 25 AND g.parallax_over_error >= 5)
ORDER BY g.source_id
```
